How a visit works

Four steps, no surprises

You will know the diagnosis, the plan, the number of sessions and the cost before anything begins.

1

Consultation

Dr. Lal examines the skin or scalp and takes your history — medication, diet, family pattern, past treatments.

2

Diagnosis

Clinical examination with dermoscopy and, where needed, blood work — so the cause is confirmed, not assumed.

3

Your plan

Sessions, timeline, home care and total cost explained upfront. You decide with the full picture in front of you.

4

Follow-up

Progress reviewed at set intervals and the plan adjusted, with maintenance advice so results hold.

Questions

Before you book

Who will I be seeing?

Dr. Ankur Lal — MBBS, MD Dermatology with a Gold Medal, trained at AIIMS New Delhi. He handles the consultation and performs the procedures himself, including hair transplants and injectables.

Do I need an appointment?

An appointment is recommended so you are not waiting. Call or WhatsApp +91 99716 96703, or send the form on this page and the clinic will call you back to confirm a slot.

How much does treatment cost?

It depends entirely on the diagnosis and how many sessions are needed. Dr. Lal explains the full plan and the total cost at the consultation, before anything starts. Call the clinic for current consultation fees.

Are the treatments safe for Indian skin?

Yes. Peels, lasers and pigmentation protocols are selected specifically for Indian skin types, where the wrong settings can cause pigment to rebound. That is a large part of what the consultation is for.

How long does hair transplant recovery take?

Most people return to routine work within a few days. Transplanted hair sheds first and regrows over several months, with visible density usually from the fourth month onwards. Dr. Lal will give you a timeline for your specific case at the consultation.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Common childhood skin problems including allergies, eczema, rashes and fungal infections are treated at the clinic.
